2025.05 – 08internship

Finding the right diagram in construction PDFs

AI System Developer Intern · Ymbia, Miami, FL

I designed an AI workflow that finds specific diagrams in long construction PDFs, saving clients from searching through messy documents. There were setbacks at the start, but instead of guessing, I studied how Docling and large language models read PDFs and discovered that icons, seals and logos were confusing the model. After I built a feedback loop to discard those images, results rose sharply. I then tested the system on 20 documents and reported to the developers that four categories needed better labeled data: doors, windows, glass types and hinges.

2024.08 – 2026.04donated

An RFID meal-plan system for my high school

Lead Developer and Co-President · Computer Science Club, San Miguelito, Panama

At my high school in Panama, some students spent half of their lunch break waiting in line. I led more than 30 members in building an RFID meal-plan system. When the vice principal and cafeteria manager were concerned about our project's scale, I mapped the cafeteria on site and narrowed the problem to the cashier line, where the delay began. We met the staff every month, and their feedback simplified the interface and corrected our understanding of meal-plan quotas. With older, experienced students mentoring beginners in small teams, we finished the system and donated it to the school.

IPO Lockup Tracker, checked by hand

Personal project

A tracker that estimates when early holders of a US IPO can sell after the lock-up period, built on SEC EDGAR filings. I checked its output by hand against the filings. Holder data held up, but two of five lock-up outputs were wrong, and the confidence score did not separate right rows from wrong ones. Those errors are not fixed yet; checking by hand is how I found them.
